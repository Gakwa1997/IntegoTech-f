Questions

What clients usually ask us

We already have someone who handles our computers. Why would we need you?

Most organisations have someone capable who fixes machines when they break. That is repair, not technology management — and it is certainly not system building. We handle the parts that rarely get done: the website, the management platform, verified backups, security and access reviews, licence optimisation, documentation and forward planning. In many cases we work alongside your existing technician rather than replacing them — and we train them.

Can you really deliver this remotely, from another country?

Yes, and most of our work already is. Cloud administration, identity and security configuration, system development, website delivery and advisory work require no physical presence. We state our working hours in UTC, collaborate through Teams and shared documentation, hold to a four-hour response standard, and report in writing every week. On-site presence is arranged where it genuinely adds value — audits, installations, staff training.

Is the audit genuinely free?

Yes. We review every system and give you a written report with findings and recommendations. You keep the report whether or not you engage us. We do this because in our experience organisations underestimate their exposure — particularly around backups and access control — and the report makes the decision obvious either way.

Do we own our systems, or are we locked into you?

You own everything. Domains are registered in your name, admin credentials are held by you, source code and configuration for custom systems are handed over, and every system we build is documented. Partnerships are monthly with 30 days' notice. We would rather keep clients because the service is good than because leaving is painful.

How do you handle our data and security?

Security is designed in, not added afterwards. MFA and least-privilege access are standard on every build. Every system we deliver includes role-based access and audit trails. Backups are encrypted and restores are tested rather than assumed. We hold only the access required to do the work, and that access is documented and revocable by you at any time.

Can you help us attract more students or customers online?

Yes — this sits across Pillars 1 and 3. A professional website, correct Google Business and Maps listing, working enquiry forms, search visibility and an active social presence measurably affect enrolment and enquiries. For organisations seeking international students, customers or partnerships, we also prepare the digital presentation that those audiences expect.
